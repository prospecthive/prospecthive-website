How a list gets made

A simple process that gets you clean contacts quickly.

  1. Understand

    You tell us who you want to reach: industry, company size, location and job titles.

  2. Research

    We find the companies and the right decision makers for that audience.

  3. Verify

    We confirm each person, their role and their email address. Contacts that fail are removed.

  4. Deliver

    You receive the list in Excel, CSV or directly in your CRM.
