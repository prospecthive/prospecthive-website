Common questions

Short answers about how our lists, checks and free samples work.

What is a B2B prospect list?

A B2B prospect list is a file of businesses and the decision makers at them, with job titles and contact details, built for a specific audience. Sales teams and agencies load it into their outreach tools or CRM to contact the right people.

What does ProspectHive do?

ProspectHive is a B2B lead generation agency. We research prospects, find and check business emails, do LinkedIn prospecting, clean CRM records, and help book appointments for sales teams.

How do you verify contacts?

We confirm each person, their role and their email address before delivery. Contacts that fail these checks are removed from the list, so you only receive records that passed.

What format do I receive the list in?

Lists come as an Excel or CSV file, or we can load them directly into your CRM.

Can I get a free sample first?

Yes. Tell us who you want to reach and we will send a free sample of 25 contacts built for that audience, with no commitment.

Do you work with lead generation and cold email agencies?

Yes. We work as a white-label research partner. You send the ideal customer profile for a client, and we return a checked list you can send under your own brand.
