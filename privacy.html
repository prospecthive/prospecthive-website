Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

ProspectHive is a B2B lead generation agency. You can reach us at prospecthive.agency@gmail.com.

What we collect on this website

  • The details you type into the enquiry form: your name, work email, company and what you want to reach.
  • Anything you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical data. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services may see your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your request, send a sample list, and deliver our service. We do not sell your details.

Who else handles it

Form submissions are delivered to our email inbox through a form service. Email and WhatsApp are handled by their own providers under their own policies.

Business contact data we provide

Our service provides business contact information about professionals, such as names, job titles, company names and business email addresses, for B2B outreach. If you want your business contact details removed from our lists, email us from the address in question and we will remove it.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email prospecthive.agency@gmail.com and we will respond.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
